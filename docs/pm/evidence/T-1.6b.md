- Result: **no top-10 changes for any of 30 stat/mode/scope combinations.** NOT checked: playoff leaders.
- records table: 54 rows, all single_game from player_game; 0 belong to a gap player. No single-season or career record rows exist yet (candidates would be unaffected per the above).
