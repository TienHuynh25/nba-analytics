## Planted short day: why "NOT caught" is expected

Log line (docs/pm/evidence/T-1.8-6.log): `planted short day: NOT caught`. That log does not print the planted day's size or count. The numbers below come from T-1.8-7.log, a read-only re-run on the cached 2025-26 schedule and game logs (no network, no file writes; script in the QA scratchpad).

Rule: `check_game_counts` (ingest/incremental.py:141-157) flags a day when `abs(fetched - scheduled) / scheduled > tolerance`. The tolerance is 0.10 (config/sources.yaml:10, `row_count_tolerance`). The edge is inclusive.

Printed output (docs/pm/evidence/T-1.8-7.log):
```
busiest scheduled day: 2026-04-10 with 15 scheduled games
UNPLANTED real data, all scheduled days: no flags
  busiest day observed: 15 of 15 scheduled
  missing | observed | deviation | tolerance | result
        0 |       15 |     0.00% |     10.0% | NOT flagged
        1 |       14 |     6.67% |     10.0% | NOT flagged
        2 |       13 |    13.33% |     10.0% | FLAGGED (game counts off schedule: 2026-04-10: fetched 13, scheduled 15)
        3 |       12 |    20.00% |     10.0% | FLAGGED (game counts off schedule: 2026-04-10: fetched 12, scheduled 15)
smallest caught deficit on 2026-04-10: 2 missing (13.33%)
```

So on the busiest day (15 games, 2026-04-10), a deficit of 1 game (6.67%) is within tolerance and is NOT caught. The smallest caught deficit is 2 games (13.33%).

Tests that pin this behaviour:
- `tests/test_anomaly_check.py:56` `test_tolerance_boundary`: unit boundary. Fetched 9 and 11 against scheduled 10 (exactly 10%) pass; fetched 8 and 12 (20%) fail.
- `tests/test_anomaly_check.py:184-197` (`test_e2e_short_day_stops_before_build_and_publish` and `test_e2e_day_at_the_tolerance_edge_reaches_publish` at :192): end-to-end through `refresh.nightly`. A 2-of-10 short day stops before build and publish, and an exactly-10% day reaches publish.

Which short days ARE caught: any day with more than 10% of its games missing. On 15 games that means 2 or more. On 10 games, 2 missing (20%) is caught and 1 missing (10%) passes. A scheduled day with nothing fetched is also caught (`test_scheduled_day_with_nothing_fetched_fails`).

Known gap, R-001 (docs/pm/RISKS.md): the pure ±10% rule lets one missing game on a slate of 10 or more pass. The owner question on an absolute floor is open. It was not added because the rule matches the spec and config.
