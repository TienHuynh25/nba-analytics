## D-025 handling

- Ties: ranked answers use `RANK() <= limit`, so every row tied at the cut is kept, and ties are listed by id or date. Q40, Q42 and Q75 carry the tie rule in `gold.rule`. On this snapshot their top rows are unique: Warriors 73-9, Klay Thompson 14, OG Anunoby 88.
- Row order: `ordered: true` only for rankings and series. Compare cases (Q23–Q30, Q78 and others) are unordered.
- Floats are stored at 6 decimals and scored under the registry rounding. The run checks that the 6-decimal value shows the same as the raw one. Q62 needs no AVG (per-season values). In Q72 the AVG is context and is rounded in SQL.
