# Net rating

Net rating is a team's point differential per 100 possessions: offensive rating minus defensive
rating.

**Formula:** Net rating = ORtg − DRtg

- Offensive rating (ORtg) is points scored per 100 possessions.
- Defensive rating (DRtg) is points allowed per 100 possessions.
- A positive net rating means the team outscores opponents per possession. Because it is per
  possession, it compares fast and slow teams fairly.

**Caveats:** values are NBA.com's published ratings (1996-97 on).
