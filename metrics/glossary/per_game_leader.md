# Qualifying for per-game league leaders

To qualify for a per-game league leaderboard, such as the scoring title, a player must appear in
at least 70% of his team's games. In an 82-game season that is 58 games.

A player with fewer games can still be listed as the leader if his season total, divided by that
minimum number of games, would still be the highest in the league.

Percentage leaderboards use made-shot minimums instead. For example, the 3-point percentage leader
needs at least 82 made 3-pointers.

This is the league-leader rule. It is separate from the 65-game minimum for awards such as MVP.
