# Player Efficiency Rating (PER)

Player Efficiency Rating is a per-minute rating of a player's overall statistical production,
created by basketball analyst John Hollinger. It adds positive box-score contributions (points,
rebounds, assists, steals, blocks, made shots) and subtracts negative ones (missed shots,
turnovers, fouls), then adjusts for team pace and scales the result so the league average is 15.

**Caveats:** NBA.com does not publish PER, and this assistant does not compute it. PER favors
high-usage scorers and measures little of a player's defense. Use the published advanced stats
(usage rate, true shooting percentage, net rating) for numbers.
