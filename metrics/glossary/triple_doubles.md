# Triple-double

A triple-double is a game in which a player reaches 10 or more in at least three of five
categories: points, rebounds, assists, steals and blocks. Most triple-doubles combine points,
rebounds and assists. A double-double is 10 or more in at least two of those categories.

**Caveats:** counts here come from box scores. NBA.com box scores are partial before 1996-97, and
steals and blocks were not tracked before 1973-74. So career counts for earlier players come from
the curated records table.
